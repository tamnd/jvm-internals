## Why the noes

Every route that was refused, in the words it was refused with. A pid in a message is the pid of a target the probe started and then killed.

- `core_self` on `linux-x64-root`: core_pattern pipes to /usr/share/apport/apport -p%p -s%s -c%c -d%d -P%P -u%u -g%g -F%F -- %E
- `attach_running` on `linux-x64`: ERROR: ptrace(PTRACE_ATTACH, ..) failed for 1004977: Operation not permitted
- `jhsdb_jstack` on `linux-x64`: ERROR: ptrace(PTRACE_ATTACH, ..) failed for 1005746: Operation not permitted
- `attach_running` on `osx-arm64`: ERROR: attach: task_for_pid(31752) failed: '(os/kern) failure' (5)
- `attach_spawned` on `osx-arm64`: ERROR: attach: task_for_pid(32008) failed: '(os/kern) failure' (5)
- `attach_ptracer_any` on `osx-arm64`: prctl(PR_SET_PTRACER) is Linux only
- `core_self` on `osx-arm64`: no core appeared. core goes to /cores/core.%P, the target's limit was raised, this process sees 0/9223372036854775807, /cores is writable
- `jhsdb_jstack` on `osx-arm64`: ERROR: attach: task_for_pid(32167) failed: '(os/kern) failure' (5)
